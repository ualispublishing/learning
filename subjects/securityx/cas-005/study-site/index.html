One repeatable system

Study workflow

Use the site as a loop, not a library. Each stage has a specific job.

01

First pass

Move through new cards objective by objective. Retrieve the front before revealing Layer 1, then open deeper layers only where your explanation is incomplete.

02

Spaced retrieval

Clear the due queue daily. Grade the quality of retrieval, not familiarity. Wrong and Hard answers become tomorrow’s highest-value work.

03

Interleaved application

Use mixed scenarios across domains. Record confidence before answering; low-confidence correct answers still belong in the weak queue.

04

Performance practice

Work PBQs, CLI/log-analysis tasks, diagrams and labs without answer cues. Explain tradeoffs and evidence, not just the final control name.

05

Error-driven refinement

Revisit missed objectives after a delay. The dashboard prioritizes lapses, low-confidence quiz answers and incomplete PBQs automatically.
