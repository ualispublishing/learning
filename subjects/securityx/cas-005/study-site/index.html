Core loop after first pass

  1. Due layered flashcards.
  2. 20–30 mixed hard scenarios.
  3. Review every miss and low-confidence correct answer.
  4. One lab, architecture diagram, log-analysis task, or PBQ block.
  5. Convert each real mistake into one retrievable rule/card/action.
