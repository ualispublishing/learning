# CISSP Atlas — Start Here Tomorrow

Use this sequence the first time you open the site. Do not begin by reading everything.

1. **Run the 16-question diagnostic once.** It uses two original scenarios per domain and is only for routing your first study block.
2. **Open the weakest domain in Learn.** Retrieve the answer before revealing it; grade the first retrieval honestly.
3. **Use Layer 4 when you miss or hesitate.** The misconception layer is where many CISSP distinctions become exam-useful.
4. **Use the precision-depth cards.** They cover commonly confused areas such as risk ownership, sanitization, PKI, IPsec, federation, Kerberos, audit/testing distinctions, recovery models, forensics, and secure development.
5. **Run 10–20 mixed scenarios.** For every answer, state why the selected option is best and why at least one plausible distractor loses.
6. **Repair misses by objective, not by memorizing the question.** The site routes back to weak objectives and keeps spaced-review state locally.
7. **Stop after a clean review block.** Let the scheduler bring cards back later instead of immediately repeating them until recognition replaces recall.

## CISSP decision order when two answers both look plausible

Use scenario evidence first, then prefer the answer that best preserves this order when applicable:

- protect people, ethics, and legal obligations;
- clarify business requirement, scope, and accountable owner;
- assess risk before buying or implementing a tool;
- choose governance/process/design before tactical implementation when the question asks what should happen first;
- apply least privilege, separation of duties, defense in depth, and explicit trust boundaries;
- preserve evidence and business continuity during incidents;
- verify that the control actually works and monitor residual risk.

This is a decision lens, not a universal answer key.

## Suggested first study block

- Diagnostic: 16 questions.
- Weak-domain retrieval: 15–25 cards.
- Mixed application: 10 questions.
- Repair: review only the objectives you missed or graded 1/2.
- Finish by checking Progress and leaving due reviews scheduled.
