# CISSP Atlas — Start Here

Use this sequence the first time you open the current release. Do not begin by reading everything.

1. **Run the 16-question diagnostic once** if you do not already have a baseline. It is a routing signal, not an exam-readiness score.
2. **Open the weakest domain in Learn.** Retrieve the answer before revealing it and grade the first retrieval honestly.
3. **Use Layer 4 when you miss or hesitate.** The misconception layer is where many CISSP distinctions become exam-useful.
4. **Use the precision-depth cards** for commonly confused areas such as risk ownership, sanitization, PKI, IPsec, federation, Kerberos, audit/testing distinctions, recovery, forensics, and secure development.
5. **Run 10–20 standard scenarios with `Exam + Stretch` selected.** Commit Low/Medium/High confidence before choosing an answer.
6. **Repair high-confidence misses first.** They are stronger misconception signals than uncertain misses.
7. **Read all four option rationales.** Learn why the best option wins and why plausible alternatives lose; do not memorize wording.
8. **Use a Bellringer only after a normal study block.** It is an explicitly non-exam-representative integrative drill.
9. **Stop cleanly and re-test later.** Use spacing instead of immediately repeating until recognition replaces recall.

## CISSP decision order when two answers both look plausible

Use scenario evidence first, then prefer the answer that best preserves this order when applicable:

- protect people, ethics, and legal obligations;
- clarify business requirement, scope, and accountable owner;
- assess risk before buying or implementing a tool;
- choose governance/process/design before tactical implementation when the question asks what should happen first;
- apply least privilege, separation of duties, defense in depth, and explicit trust boundaries;
- preserve evidence and business continuity during incidents;
- verify that the control actually works and monitor residual risk.

This is a decision lens, not a universal answer key.

## Suggested study block

- Diagnostic, if no baseline exists: 16 questions.
- Weak-domain retrieval: 15–25 cards.
- Calibrated application: 10–20 Exam + Stretch questions.
- Repair: review objectives behind misses, especially high-confidence misses.
- Optional: one Bellringer case.
- Finish in Progress and leave due reviews scheduled.
