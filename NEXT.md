## Next
▶ **Introduce Yourself in Arabic — University of Wisconsin–Madison**

- URL: https://wisc.pb.unizin.org/lctlresources/chapter/introduce-yourself-in-arabic/
- Medium: recorded interactive lesson
- Variety: Modern Standard Arabic
- Role: core listening + production
- Goal: say hello, introduce yourself, state where you are from, and ask another person their name/origin.

This is intentionally the next step because greetings are already in your completed concept history. We are not restarting the alphabet or immediately repeating greetings.

### Before starting
From memory, say:
- one greeting;
- how you are;
- one morning/evening greeting;
- how you would address one person vs a group.

Then attempt **اسمي ...** before opening the lesson, even if you are unsure.

### Learning protocol
1. Listen to each recording before turning to the answer/translation where possible.
2. Repeat aloud and shadow the recording.
3. Retrieve the phrase from memory.
4. Personalize it with your actual name/origin.
5. End by giving a 30–60 second mini-introduction without reading.
